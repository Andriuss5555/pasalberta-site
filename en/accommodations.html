Accommodations

Our estate has three houses:

Sauna House 1

A cottage with a mini kitchen, party hall and sauna on the first floor. The second floor has beds with the possibility to accommodate up to 15-17 people.

Sauna House 2

As comfortable as the first one, just slightly smaller: with a sauna, kitchenette and sleeping places on the second floor for up to 11-13 people.

* This cottage is rented less frequently, mostly to returning guests who come just to warm up in the sauna, without overnight stay. :)

Large Event House

This house has a spacious event hall that accommodates up to 44 people, 13 rooms with the possibility to accommodate up to 32 guests. A large, fully equipped kitchen. Perfect for larger events and family celebrations.
