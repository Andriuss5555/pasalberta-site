About Us

The "PasAlberta" estate is located in a peaceful natural setting, where every visitor can find an oasis of rest and tranquility. Our goal is to provide you with a comfortable vacation with the opportunity to escape from the city noise.

We are a family business that has been cherishing traditions and hospitality for many years. We offer comfortable cottages, sauna, outdoor activities and a friendly environment for rest as a couple, family or group of friends.

With us you will find not only accommodation, but also a place for active recreation!
