Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can we listen to loud music all night?

Of course! We have no objections to that. But we always recommend doing it inside the buildings, although we are in a fairly private area, we still have neighbors who live relatively close :)

Can we jump into the pond?

Again a positive answer. The pond is dug with a fairly steep slope, and just 1-1.5m from the end of the ladder the depth reaches about 2 meters, the deepest places depending on the season can be 4+ meters :)

When do we need to leave the premises?

We are always flexible and it's not difficult to arrange, but if we are expecting other guests on your departure day, 12:00 is the latest we can let you stay - beyond that we need to manage to clean up and prepare for others :)

Do you take a deposit / reservation fee?

Yes. For the sauna house we apply a 50 EUR deposit, and for the event house 100 EUR deposit, which we treat as confirmation that you will actually participate and we cross out these dates from the calendar. Also, this money is "frozen" for any potential damage to our property. So after each party, if nothing was broken, etc., you get this money back :)

How much does the estate rental cost???

Probably the most frequent and most awkward question we receive, ALL OUR PRICES ARE IN THE PRICE LIST. ALL OUR PRICES ARE TRANSPARENT!!!!
Preparing for a wedding? Great! Congratulations! The estate price is as you see in the price list :)
Preparing for a christening? CONGRATULATIONS! The price is EXACTLY as you see in the price list!
We know how "crafty" our nation can be and prices are, to put it mildly, adjusted according to the nature of the celebration, well, no matter how annoying it may be, we are all equal and we're not going to make your wallets any thinner because of that :)))

And if you have additional questions, we will always answer them!
